**EchoOffGrid • first development preview**

What can you still open when the Wi-Fi goes off? EchoOffGrid puts Wikipedia, a dictionary, maps and a media shelf on your Mac or Android phone. The preview packages include a starter library you can try on first launch.

If you try it, go offline, search Wikipedia for *ocean*, then play a video. We'd love to hear which device you used and how those two things felt.

Today you can sync a paired Mac and phone over USB or local Wi-Fi. Next up is peer-to-peer exchange and testing small messages over mesh networks.
